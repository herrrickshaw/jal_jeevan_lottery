## Detailed Operational Workflow

### Ticket Journey (Start to End)

```
DAY 1 (Printing):
  └─ Lottery board approves serial numbers
  └─ Printer applies security (hologram, UV ink, NFC)
  └─ Blockchain registers serial + secure hash
  └─ Tickets packed (1,000/box) + sealed

DAY 2-3 (Distribution):
  └─ Vendors receive tickets via secure courier
  └─ Biometric sign-off (tamper prevention)
  └─ POS system confirms receipt
  
DAY 4-7 (Sales):
  └─ Citizen buys ticket (₹50 for Green)
  └─ Vendor scans QR code (SMS sent: "Ticket active")
  └─ Buyer receives receipt + project link
  └─ Blockchain updates ownership (Aadhaar-linked)
  
DAY 8 (Draw Preparation):
  └─ Lottery board seals draw machines
  └─ Independent auditor certifies
  └─ Draw scheduled for Friday 6 PM
  
DAY 8 (Draw):
  └─ Live broadcast begins (YouTube + DD Kisan)
  └─ RNG selects 5-6 winning serials (per draw rules)
  └─ Winners announced in real-time
  └─ App notification sent to winners
  
DAY 9-14 (Claim):
  └─ Winner submits claim (online for ≤₹10k)
  └─ Verification complete (48-72 hours)
  └─ Prize credited to bank account
  └─ TDS (30%) deducted at source
  
PARALLEL (Project Tracking):
  └─ ₹50k prize amount allocated to water project
  └─ GPS coordinates sent to winner
  └─ Weekly photos/videos of construction
  └─ Completion within 6 months
  └─ Winner invited to inauguration ceremony
```

## Risk Mitigation Plan

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|-----------|
| **Political resistance** from incumbent lottery operators | High | High | Revenue-sharing agreement + transition period (3 years) |
| **Addiction escalation** | Medium | High | Strict caps (₹2k/month) + helpline + education |
| **Ticket counterfeiting** | Medium | Medium | Blockchain + NFC + physical holograms |
| **Low uptake** in Year 1 | Low | High | Celebrity endorsement + ₹500 Cr marketing |
| **Project delays** in fund deployment | Medium | Medium | Direct vendor partnerships + penalty clauses |
| **IT outages** during draw | Low | Critical | Backup systems + decentralized blockchain |
| **Legal challenges** (constitutionality) | Low | High | Pre-approval from AG + SC-ready defense |

## Success Criteria (Go/No-Go Gates)

### End of Phase 1 (Month 3): Go/No-Go Decision

**Proceed if**:
- ✓ 50+ Crore tickets sold in pilot
- ✓ Public sentiment: >65% positive
- ✓ Zero major security breaches
- ✓ Draw integrity certified by auditor
- ✓ ₹112.5 Cr transferred to JJM without delay

**Pause if**:
- ✗ Ticket sales <20 Crore
- ✗ Addiction complaints >100 per week
- ✗ Counterfeiting detected (>1% of tickets)
- ✗ Legal challenges filed (state vs. central)

- ✓ 12-state revenue: ₹3,000 Crores
