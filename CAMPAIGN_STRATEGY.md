## II. Regional Campaign Frameworks

### Tier-1 Campaign: Rural Focus (Green Ticket)
**Target States**: Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Odisha  
**Reach**: 200+ million people

#### **Message Architecture**

**Primary Message**:
> "₹50 Ticket = 1 Life Changes. Your Win = Village Gets Water."

**Sub-messages**:
1. **Functional benefit**: "No more 3-hour daily walk. Water at your door."
2. **Health angle**: "₹50 keeps cholera out. Wins change everything."
3. **Women empowerment**: "Mothers' wish: Tap water. Your ticket makes it happen."
4. **Gamification**: "Play twice a week. Win ₹50,000. Fund 2 families' water."

#### **Creative Assets**

**TV Spots** (30-sec, regional languages):
```
Scene 1: Woman walking 3km for water (sunrise)
Scene 2: She buys lottery ticket (playful music begins)
Scene 3: She checks mobile app — ticket scanned
Scene 4: Same village, next week — tap at house
Scene 5: "₹50 lottery ticket. One winner. Two taps."
CTA: "Buy today. Change tomorrow."
```

**Radio Jingles** (30-sec, state-language mix):
```
[Local folk melody]
Lyrics (Hindi example):
"Paani ho ya lottery
Dono milega teri
Ticket lo mere bhai
Ghar mein nalon di haai"

Translation: "Water or lottery—you get both, brother.
Buy a ticket. Taps come to your house."
```

**Social Media Content**:
- Before/After photos (real villages post-water)
- User testimonials (video TikTok/Instagram Reels: 15-sec)
- Live draw countdown (Instagram Stories: poll participation)
- Beneficiary impact stories (Facebook: weekly feature)

#### **Distribution Channels**

| Channel | Format | Frequency | Reach |
|---------|--------|-----------|-------|

## III. Milestone-Based Campaigns

### Campaign: "5 Crore Milestone Achieved" (Trigger: National Event)

#### **Timeline**: When national connection count hits 50 million

**Message**: "India Just Hit 50 Million Taps. Celebrate. Win Big."

**Activation**:
```
Phase 1 (Day 1): News Conference
  - PM/minister announces milestone
  - Live broadcast (all channels)
  - Lottery authority live announces: ₹1 Crore bonus draw
  - Media coverage: 50M+ impressions

Phase 2 (Days 2-7): Celebration Campaign
  - "Golden Week" draw (double prize pool all 7 days)
  - TV marathons (morning + prime time)
  - Social media: Milestone celebration TikToks (user-generated)
  - Hashtag trend: #50CroreTapsIndia #JalJeevanWin

Phase 3 (Days 8-30): Gratitude Campaign
  - Video montage: beneficiary testimonials
  - Impact report: "How your ₹50 became ₹50,000 and changed 2 homes"
  - Incentive: Next 30 days, buy ₹500 = bonus ₹100 credit
```

**Expected Reach**: 200M+ impressions, 30M+ ticket sales spike

## VIII. Crisis Communication Strategy

### Potential Crisis: "Rigged Draw Allegations"

**Response Protocol**:
1. **Hour 1**: Official statement (denies allegation, commits to investigation)
2. **Hour 6**: Live draw replay (proof on YouTube + official channels)
3. **Day 1**: Third-party audit initiation (Big 4 firm)
4. **Day 7**: Full investigation report + compensation (if error found)
5. **Ongoing**: Transparency dashboard (weekly updates)

### Potential Crisis: "Addiction Impact Stories"

**Response Protocol**:
1. Immediate helpline statement (1-800-JJM-HELP running)
2. Public commitment to stricter caps + self-exclusion
3. Celebrity PSA: "If you're struggling, reach out"
4. Quarterly impact report (% addicted individuals helped)
