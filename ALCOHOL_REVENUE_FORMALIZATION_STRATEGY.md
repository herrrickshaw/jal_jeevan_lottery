## The TASMAC Corruption Case: A System Failure

### DVAC Investigation Summary (₹1,000 Cr Scam)

**Allegations** (as of news report):
1. **Tender manipulation** in transport contracts (supply chain)
2. **Cartelisation** by liquor manufacturers + TASMAC officials
3. **Kickbacks** to state minister + bureaucrats
4. **MRP violation** (retail overpricing, customers charged above stated price)
5. **License irregularities** (bars given preferential licenses for bribes)
6. **Underreporting of sales** (actual sales > reported sales = tax loss)

**Locations raided**: 41 sites across Tamil Nadu (distilleries, TASMAC offices, bars, private residences)

**Perpetrators** (sample):
- Former Transport Minister V Senthil Balaji
- TASMAC officials
- Liquor manufacturers
- Retail bar operators
- IAS officer S Visakan

**Revenue Loss to State**:
- Tax evasion on unreported sales: ₹400–500 Cr/year
- Retail overpricing margin: ₹300–400 Cr/year (customer → bar owner → official)
- License fraud: ₹100–200 Cr/year (preferential allocations)
- Transport cartel markup: ₹50–100 Cr/year
- **Total annual leak**: ₹850–1,200 Cr (Tamil Nadu alone)

**Extrapolated nationally**: ₹6,000–8,000 Cr/year in corruption + tax evasion (14 major states: TN, Delhi, MH, KA, AP, Punjab, etc.)

## Where the Money Leaks Today

### Corruption Pathways in Current System

```
Bottle sold at retail (₹100 bill)
    ↓
MRP = ₹80, actual charge = ₹100
    ↓ (₹20 overcharge)
Bar owner keeps ₹10 (margin)
Official gets ₹5 (bribe)
TASMAC loses ₹5 (tax on ₹20)
    ↓
Customer receives underreported receipt (or no receipt)
TASMAC sales records don't match actual inventory
    ↓
State excise dept cannot audit → corruption perpetuates
```

### Three Leakage Points

**1. Underreporting (₹250–350 Cr/year nationally)**
- Bar owners underreport sales volume (1 bottle sold = 0.5 bottles reported)
- Manual inventory tracking (easy to falsify)
- No digital receipt requirement (customer gets chit, not formal bill)
- Tax evasion: bar × actual sales mismatch

**2. Overpricing (₹300–400 Cr/year nationally)**
- MRP printed on bottle is violated (bar charges ₹150 for ₹120 MRP bottle)
- Customer complaint redressal = non-existent
- Official complicity (shop license tied to unofficial payments)
- Margin capture: 15–25% above MRP

**3. License Cartels (₹150–200 Cr/year nationally)**
- Limited bar licenses issued → scarcity creates rent-seeking
- Licenses allocated to connected operators (bribe = ₹50–100 Lakh per location)
- Renewal tied to unofficial payments
- Concentration = reduced competition = price inflation
